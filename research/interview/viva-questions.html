Common Viva Questions

Basic interview questions frequently asked in research institute recruitment viva boards.

Introduction

Interview boards often begin with basic academic background, subject knowledge, and personal motivation questions.

Common Questions

  • Tell us about yourself.
  • Why do you want to join this institute?
  • What is your academic specialization?
  • What are your strengths in agriculture-related subjects?
  • How will you contribute to the institute if selected?

Preparation Tip

Prepare short, confident, and clear answers. Focus on subject knowledge, communication ability, and practical understanding.
